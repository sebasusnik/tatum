A WAV of a record: its hits are averaged on the beat grid, so what comes with every hit is kept and the rest thins out. Pick a stretch where the kick plays alone or nearly (an intro, an outro), and only as many ms as come before the bass. An MP3 has to be a WAV first: ffmpeg -i song.mp3 song.wav.
