# Practice sheet: hiperespacio with the TUI

## How to start

```
tatum set play sets/hiperespacio --auto --tui
```

- `--auto`: the set moves on by itself, as in `hiperespacio-tocado.mp3`. You only play the gestures; A8/A7 still jump if you want.
- **Under the steps, the TUI shows a practice line:**
  - `bar 17/24 · ●●○○` says which bar of the step you are in, and which beat.
  - `▶ ...` (highlighted) is the gesture for this bar.
  - `next: in 3 beats · ...` is the one coming. It turns gold when less than a bar is left.
- **To practice one track alone:** pick the step with ← → and press Enter to go (it lands on the next line). For example, the grinder is step 70.
- **Quantized pads cover your aim:** A1 lands on the bar; B1, B6 and A3 on the sixteenth; B2, B3 and B4 on the beat. Press a little early, never late.

## Legend

- **A1** kick out · **A3** throw · **A4** freeze · **A5** roll 1/8 · **B1** signature · **B2** riser · **B3** crash · **B4** figure · **B5** roll 1/32 · **B6** signature into the echo.
- **K3** is the signature's RPM/character knob. "K3 to center" = leave it centered when you let go.
- "Held" = keep it down until "let go". "Tap" = press and release.

## Practice plan

1. **Only the crashes (B3) and the kick-outs (A1)** through a whole hour: 16 gestures (10 crashes and 6 kick-outs) that teach you to feel the one.
2. **Add the throws (A3)**, always on the last half bar of a phrase.
3. **Add the signatures (B1 + K3)**, one track at a time.
4. **Last, the builds** (B2 + B5 + B3), the 5 big moments.
